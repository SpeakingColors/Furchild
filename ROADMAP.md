# Furchild roadmap

Done in v0.1.0 (test build): native JUCE port of the approved mockup, 2x oversampled tube stage and soft ceiling, 16 factory presets, installers and manual.

Next
- Listening tests and tuning by ear (the variable-mu law, tube amount and the Level Match speed are our own choices, measured but not yet judged by ear).
- Optional 4x mode for the soft ceiling (hard driven it still aliases about -24 dB at 15 kHz; see the manual, Under the hood).
- Test the installers and the Flatpak extension on real machines (Windows, macOS, a Flatpak DAW) and in several hosts.
- Resize and HiDPI checks in real hosts.

Not planned: any network feature, telemetry or account.
